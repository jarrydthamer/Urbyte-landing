The Market Problem

Bandwidth is perishable.
Your unused capacity is gone forever.

Telecom networks provision for peak demand. What isn't consumed during a billing cycle cannot be stored, rolled back, or recovered. It evaporates — every month, at scale — as deadweight loss on your infrastructure investment. Enterprises compound the problem by over-provisioning fleet plans by an estimated 30% just to avoid overage charges. Telecom audit firms routinely recover 25–35% of total enterprise spend by eliminating unused services. That money was never captured. It was simply lost.

Stakeholder Waste Problem Economic Impact
Consumers Pay for data they don't use. Unused data vanishes at the end of each billing cycle. "Rollover" plans simply defer the loss — they don't eliminate it. Persistent value destruction driving high churn as subscribers seek plans that feel "fairer."
Enterprises Over-provision corporate fleet plans to avoid overage charges. 25–35% of total telecom spend is routinely identified as recoverable waste by audit firms. Millions in annual deadweight loss. No mechanism to recapture, redirect, or offset that value.
Wholesale Carriers Provision capacity based on peak demand. Off-peak bandwidth is structurally unmonetised — it cannot be stored and is never billed. Lost revenue, inefficient capital expenditure, and rising churn from subscribers who feel underserved.
System Architecture

Five components.
One infrastructure layer.

Urbyte operates entirely outside the core network. No impact on latency, no changes to wholesale pricing, no disruption to existing contracts. We connect via read-only, mTLS-secured APIs — ingesting, verifying, and converting waste into a cryptographically signed asset.

Urbyte Insight — Ingestion & Verification

Securely ingests anonymised usage logs from carriers and enterprises. Calculates the Systemic Waste Delta (Δ) — the gap between allocated and consumed capacity. Normalises records without storing PII.

Δ_Waste = Allocated Capacity − Consumed Capacity Each waste event: → Signed by carrier node key → Co-signed by Urbyte oracle key → Proof of Waste (PoWst) manifest issued

DBU Ledger — Minting Protocol

Converts verified waste into Data-Backed Units. The number of DBUs minted is proportional to verified waste volume and the network's production cost index. A floor value prevents token inflation.

1 GB verified waste = 10 DBU minted 1 DBU floor value = AU $0.05 Dual signature required per manifest Intrinsic floor: wholesale cost of capacity No ceiling on accumulation

Givabit — Clearinghouse & Routing

Receives minted DBUs and routes them to ACNC-registered DGR charities or reward partners. Enterprise donations are classified as non-cash property gifts under ATO TR 2011/4 — triggering a full tax deduction at market value.

DBU → ACNC DGR register verified → ATO TR 2011/4 property gift treatment → AASB 138 intangible asset classification → No AFSL requirement triggered → Auditable DGR receipt issued
