Why the waste is structural

Carriers provision for peak demand and sell plans against an average-use assumption that underestimates consumption variance. The gap between allocation and consumption is not accidental — it is the mechanism that prevents overage charges and maintains margin. Artificial billing cycle boundaries ensure unused capacity cannot be deferred indefinitely. What is not consumed by cycle end evaporates.

Rollover plans slow the expiry. They do not eliminate it. The structural delta persists across every plan type, every carrier, every cycle.

26 million plans. 55GB average monthly delta. Expiring continuously.

Why retention is the commercial case

A DBU balance accruing over three or more billing cycles creates a measurable switching cost that operates independently of pricing — the subscriber leaves demonstrable accrued value on the table if they move. No discount strategy replicates that.

The Givabit cohort extends it further. Subscribers routing DBUs to a nominated DGR charity introduce a non-financial identity attachment to their plan. That cohort churns 51% less than the carrier baseline.

No pricing change. No contract renegotiation. A read-only feed and 90 days.

System Architecture

Six components.
One out-of-band layer.

The entire Urbyte stack operates outside the core network. No write access. No latency impact. No operational risk surface for the carrier. Full technical specifications are available to verified partners under NDA.

01 — Ingestion

Data Ingestion Engine

Securely ingests anonymised carrier usage data via a read-only, enterprise-grade API connection. Calculates the verified gap between allocated fleet capacity and actual consumption each billing cycle. Zero PII stored at any point.

02 — Verification

Waste Verification Layer

Applies Urbyte's proprietary verification protocol to each capacity event. Every verified waste record is independently auditable and cryptographically signed. Classified under accredited intangible asset accounting standards. No AFSL triggered.

03 — Distribution

Social Impact Engine

Routes verified rewards credits to ACNC-registered DGR charities or partner reward programs. Enterprise contributions qualify under ATO-compliant non-cash deduction frameworks. Auditable receipts issued per transaction.

04 — Consumer

Subscriber Interface

Retail-facing platform giving subscribers real-time visibility over their data allowance, consumption, and rewards balance. Converts unused capacity into verified rewards credits automatically at each billing cycle end.

05 — Corporate

Fleet Management Dashboard

Enterprise operator portal providing live capacity waste reporting, ESG impact metrics, charitable contribution ledger, and ATO-ready tax documentation. Full fleet telecom waste audit available on demand.

06 — Integration

Partner API

Out-of-band, read-only connection. Zero write access to carrier systems. Zero latency impact on network operations. MVNO white-label capability — full rewards infrastructure deployable under your brand with zero development overhead.
