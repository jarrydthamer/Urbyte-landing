Why It Happens

Telecoms sell data allowances — not actual data. Bandwidth is a continuously flowing resource. Artificial billing cycle caps create structural expiry: what isn't consumed by midnight on the last day of your cycle is gone forever. Carriers cannot store it. It evaporates as unrecoverable deadweight loss across every plan, every carrier, every month.

  • 26 million Australian plans expire unused data every single billing cycle
  • Average subscriber uses 14GB of a 69GB allocation — 55GB wasted monthly
  • "Rollover" plans defer the loss — they don't eliminate it
  • Carriers provision for peak demand — off-peak capacity is structurally unmonetised

Why Carriers Haven't Solved It

The waste is by design — not neglect. Overselling capacity against underuse is how telco margin works. But subscriber awareness is changing. Price sensitivity is rising. Churn is up. The model that generated margin for 20 years is now the primary driver of retention failure. Urbyte doesn't attack the model. It sits beside it — and converts the waste into loyalty.

  • Churn re-acquisition costs carriers $180–$400 per subscriber
  • DBU loyalty anchoring reduces that cost to near zero on retained accounts
  • Givabit donors — the stickiest cohort — churn 51% less than the industry baseline
  • No pricing changes, no network changes, no contract renegotiations required
System Architecture

Six components.
One infrastructure layer.

Urbyte operates entirely outside the core network. Read-only, out-of-band, mTLS-secured. No latency impact. No pricing disruption. No operational risk to the carrier. Every minting event is dual-signed and independently auditable.

01 — Ingestion

Urbyte Insight

Securely ingests anonymised CDR usage logs. Calculates the Systemic Waste Delta (Δ) — the verified gap between allocated and consumed capacity. Co-signs every event with carrier node key + Urbyte oracle key. Issues Proof of Waste (PoWst) manifest.

Δ_Waste = Allocated − Consumed (GB) Each event: carrier node key + Urbyte oracle key Output: PoWst manifest (auditable)
02 — Minting

DBU Ledger

Converts verified waste into Data-Backed Units. Proportional to verified volume and the network's production cost index. Floor value prevents inflation. No ceiling on accumulation. Classified as intangible assets under AASB 138 — no AFSL triggered.

1 GB verified waste = 10 DBU minted Floor value: AU $0.05 per unit Dual-signature required per manifest AASB 138: intangible asset class AFSL: not triggered
03 — Clearinghouse

Givabit

Routes minted DBUs to ACNC-registered DGR charities or reward partners. Enterprise donations classified as non-cash property gifts under ATO TR 2011/4 — triggering a full tax deduction at market value. Auditable DGR receipt issued per transaction.

DBU → ACNC DGR register verified → ATO TR 2011/4 (property gift) → AASB 138 classification maintained → No AFSL requirement → Auditable DGR receipt issued
04 — Consumer

Urbyte App

Mobile platform for retail subscribers. Displays real-time allowance, consumption, and DBU balance. Unused data converts to DBUs at cycle end. Redeemable for rewards or donated via Givabit. Churn anchor effect activates from DBU balance cycle 1.
