Why the waste is structural

Carriers provision for peak demand and sell plans against an average-use assumption that underestimates consumption variance. The gap between allocation and consumption is not accidental — it is the mechanism that prevents overage charges and maintains margin. Artificial billing cycle boundaries ensure unused capacity cannot be deferred indefinitely. What is not consumed by cycle end evaporates.

Rollover plans slow the expiry. They do not eliminate it. The structural delta persists across every plan type, every carrier, every cycle.

26 million plans. 55GB average monthly delta. Expiring continuously.

Why retention is the commercial case

A DBU balance accruing over three or more billing cycles creates a measurable switching cost that operates independently of pricing — the subscriber leaves demonstrable accrued value on the table if they move. No discount strategy replicates that.

The Givabit cohort extends it further. Subscribers routing DBUs to a nominated DGR charity introduce a non-financial identity attachment to their plan. That cohort churns 51% less than the carrier baseline.

No pricing change. No contract renegotiation. A read-only feed and 90 days.

System Architecture

Six components.
One out-of-band layer.

The entire Urbyte stack sits outside the core network. No write access. No latency impact. No operational risk surface for the carrier. Every minting event is dual-signed and independently auditable.

01 — Ingestion

Urbyte Insight™

Ingests anonymised CDR logs via read-only mTLS-secured API. Calculates the Unallocated Capacity Delta (Δ) — the verified gap between allocated and consumed capacity per billing cycle. Co-signs each event with carrier node key and Urbyte oracle key. Issues Proof of Waste (PoWst™) manifest.

Δ_Waste = Allocated − Consumed (GB) Carrier node key + Urbyte oracle key Output: PoWst™ manifest (auditable)
02 — Minting

DBU™ Ledger

Converts verified waste into Data-Backed Units™. Proportional to verified volume and the network production cost index. Floor value prevents inflation. No ceiling on accumulation. Classified as intangible assets under AASB 138. No AFSL triggered.

1 GB verified = 10 DBU™ minted Floor: protocol-defined floor value Dual-sig per PoWst™ manifest AASB 138 — intangible asset class
03 — Clearinghouse

Givabit™

Routes minted DBUs to ACNC-registered DGR charities or reward partners. Enterprise donations classified as non-cash property gifts under ATO TR 2011/4 — triggering a full tax deduction at market value. Auditable DGR receipt issued per transaction.

DBU → ACNC DGR register verified → ATO TR 2011/4 property gift → AASB 138 classification held → Auditable DGR receipt issued
04 — Consumer

Urbyte™ App

Retail subscriber interface. Real-time allowance, consumption, and DBU balance. Converts unused data to DBUs at cycle end. Redemption or Givabit donation from cycle 4. DBU churn anchor activates immediately from first balance accumulation.

Tiers: Bronze / Silver / Gold DBU accumulation per cycle Churn delta: −34% (DBU holders) Redemption: from cycle 4
05 — Corporate

Urbyte Corporate™

Fleet operator dashboard. Live waste streams, DBU minting volume, ESG impact metrics, charitable contribution ledger. Produces auditable ESG reports and DGR receipts for ATO tax filings. Full fleet telecom waste audit on demand.

Fleet waste: live CDR ingestion ESG receipt: per donation, auditable ATO offset: 30% on DBU market value Churn defence: monthly score update
