Revenue Architecture

A new revenue stream.
Built on existing infrastructure.

Urbyte doesn't ask telcos to change their pricing models. We monetise what's already being written off — turning structural waste into platform value for carriers and subscribers alike.

01 — CONSUMER

DBU Accumulation

Subscribers self-report unused data. Urbyte converts it to DBUs at 10 DBU per GB — $0.05 AUD each. Loyalty that costs the carrier nothing.

02 — TELCO

Churn Reduction

DBU balances create a retention anchor. Subscribers with accumulated DBUs are statistically less likely to churn — independently of pricing.

03 — MVNO

White-Label Integration

MVNOs can embed the Urbyte DBU system under their own brand via API. Full rewards infrastructure — zero development cost.

04 — PLATFORM

SaaS Licensing

Urbyte Insight provides telcos with anonymised, aggregated subscriber data intelligence — subscription-based, fully Privacy Act 1988 compliant.
