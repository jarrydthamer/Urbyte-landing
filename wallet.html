FAQ

What is the buffer period?

The first 3 months act as a verification and buffering period. This helps Urbyte avoid rushed or artificial balances while the wallet system is finalised.

Can I convert during the buffer period?

Yes. You can use the Convert page to preview your first 3 months of unused data as estimated DBUs.

Is redemption active?

No. Redemption is pending while Urbyte finalises infrastructure, verification, and member access systems.

Will members be notified before redemption opens?

Yes. Members will be notified before redemption functionality becomes active.
