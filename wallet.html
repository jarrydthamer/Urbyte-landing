Frequently Asked Questions

Why does unused value exist?
Your mobile plan includes data allocations that often go unused. We capture this value and return it to you as measurable recovery.
Why do carriers participate?
Carriers benefit from improved network insights and customer engagement. Consumer satisfaction improves when value is returned.
Is my personal data sold?
No. We operate on infrastructure-level data only. No personal browsing history, behavioural data, or identifying information is ever sold.
How are balances estimated?
Balances are calculated from verified carrier usage reports using predictive infrastructure modelling. All estimates are conservative and verifiable.
Why does recovery fluctuate?
Recovery values adjust based on actual usage patterns, allocation cycles, and network conditions. Fluctuation reflects real market dynamics.
