How It Works

Three steps from
waste to tax deduction.

Urbyte Corporate connects to your carrier's usage logs via a read-only, mTLS-secured API. No network changes. No new hardware. No disruption to your fleet. The entire process — from waste verification to tax receipt — is cryptographically auditable.

STEP 01

Waste Verified

Urbyte Insight ingests anonymised usage logs from your carrier. It calculates your Systemic Waste Delta — the gap between allocated fleet capacity and what was actually consumed. Every waste event is co-signed by your carrier's node key and Urbyte's oracle key, creating a Proof of Waste (PoWst) manifest that is independently auditable.

Δ_Waste
Allocated − Consumed = Verified waste
STEP 02

DBUs Minted

Verified waste is converted into Data-Backed Units at a rate of 10 DBU per GB. DBUs are classified as intangible assets under AASB 138 — no AFSL licensing required. A floor value reflecting the wholesale cost of producing the underlying network capacity prevents token inflation.

10 DBU / GB
Floor: AU $0.05 per unit
STEP 03

Tax Deduction Triggered

DBUs are routed via Givabit to your nominated ACNC-registered DGR charity. Under ATO TR 2011/4, the donation is classified as a non-cash property gift — triggering a tax deduction equal to the fair market value of the DBUs. An auditable DGR receipt is issued per transaction.

30% offset
ATO TR 2011/4 — property gift treatment

Legal & Compliance

Built for CFOs,
cleared by regulators.

Every component of the Urbyte Corporate platform was designed with Australian legal and tax compliance as a foundation — not an afterthought.

Tax Treatment

  • DBU donations to ACNC-registered DGR charities classified as non-cash property gifts under ATO TR 2011/4
  • Tax deduction equal to the fair market value of DBUs at time of donation
  • 30% cash tax offset at standard company tax rate — auditable, receipted, ATO-compliant
  • DBUs classified as intangible assets under AASB 138 — no AFSL licensing triggered
  • DGR compliance verified automatically via ACNC register integration

Privacy & Data Governance

  • All carrier usage logs ingested as anonymised session data — zero PII stored at any point
  • Read-only, out-of-band mTLS-secured API — no write access to carrier systems ever granted
  • Privacy Act 1988 compliant by architecture — not by policy
  • Dual cryptographic signature on every waste manifest — carrier node key + Urbyte oracle key
  • Every DBU minting transaction independently auditable — Proof of Waste (PoWst) manifest issued
