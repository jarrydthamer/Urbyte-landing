How It Works

Three steps.
Waste in. Verified impact out.

The entire process — from waste verification to ATO-compliant tax receipt — is cryptographically auditable. No network changes. No new hardware. No disruption to your fleet or existing carrier contracts.

Step 01 — Verify

Waste Captured & Signed

our enterprise analytics engine ingests anonymised carrier usage logs from your carrier via a read-only, enterprise-grade secured API. It calculates your Unallocated Capacity Delta — the gap between allocated fleet capacity and actual consumption. Every waste event is co-signed by your carrier's node key and Urbyte's oracle key, producing a our NEW_TERM waste verification protocol manifest that is independently auditable at any point.

Δ_Waste
Allocated − Consumed = Verified
Step 02 — Record

DBUs Recorded

Verified telecommunications waste may be recorded as internal Data-Backed Units under Urbyte's documented methodology. DBUs are internal platform records, not cash, cryptocurrency, legal tender or a guaranteed financial entitlement. Legal, tax and accounting treatment depends on the facts and independent professional advice.

documented recording methodology
Internal record only
Step 03 — Direct

Charitable Direction Modelled

Where a supported Givabit pathway is available, an organisation may direct an eligible DBU balance toward a nominated ACNC-registered DGR charity. Any donation, cash settlement, tax treatment or receipt must be completed separately in Australian dollars and remains subject to legal, tax, funding, charity and processor approval.
