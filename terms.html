DBUs — Data Backed Units

DBUs (Data Backed Units) are Urbyte's internal rewards credits system. They are issued by Urbyte at our discretion and governed by the following conditions:

DBUs are rewards credits issued by Urbyte. They are not currency, financial products, securities, or investments. They carry no guaranteed monetary value and cannot be exchanged directly for cash.
  • DBUs are issued based on self-reported mobile usage data submitted via your telco bill
  • The standard conversion rate is 10 DBUs per GB of verified unused data
  • DBUs may be redeemed for rewards, gift cards, or charitable donations as listed in the Urbyte platform at the time of redemption
  • DBU redemption values and available rewards are subject to change at Urbyte's discretion
  • DBUs have no expiry while your account remains active
  • DBUs are non-transferable and cannot be sold, gifted, or traded between accounts
  • DBUs are not redeemable for cash under any circumstances
  • Urbyte does not guarantee the availability of any specific reward or redemption option
04

The 3-Cycle Verification Window

Full DBU marketplace redemption value unlocks after 3 completed billing cycles. This window is a platform-side architectural constraint — it allows Urbyte to build an accurate, verified picture of a member's genuine monthly unused data average before marketplace redemption access is activated. It is not a development-stage buffer — it is a permanent platform integrity mechanism that applies to all members.

  • A billing cycle is one calendar month from your subscription start date
  • Members must submit bill data each month to receive DBU credits for that cycle
  • Failure to submit bill data in a given cycle forfeits DBU credits for that cycle only
