Conversion rules

Start date

Your 3-month buffer period starts from your next billing due date. For prepaid users, it starts from the next plan expiry or renewal date.

One cycle at a time

Only one usage cycle can be declared at a time. Month 2 cannot be submitted until Month 1 has ended.

Final-day submission

The unused amount must be declared within the final day of the usage cycle. Late declarations may be marked pending or rejected.

Verification required

A screenshot must be submitted and stored for review. It should show the carrier app usage screen.

Account match

The screenshot must display a phone number or account name that matches the member details stored in Urbyte’s system.

Privacy allowed

Unrelated private information may be blanked out. The usage amount and matching number/name must remain visible.

Conversion FAQ

When can I begin converting?

Conversion begins at your next billing due date. For prepaid users, it begins at the next plan expiry or renewal date.

Can I submit all 3 months at once?

No. Conversions must be submitted one cycle at a time.

When do I submit my unused amount?

Submit within the final day of the usage cycle, when the unused amount is known and can be verified.

What verification is needed?

A screenshot from your carrier app usage screen showing the usage amount and either your phone number or account name matching Urbyte’s member record.

Can I hide private information?

Yes. You may blank out unrelated private information. Do not hide the usage amount or matching account name/number.

Does this create live redeemable DBUs?

No. This creates a preview declaration only until Urbyte’s verification, member storage and redemption systems are live.
