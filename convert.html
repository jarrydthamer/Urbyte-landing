Conversion FAQ

Why the first 3 months?

The first 3 months are a buffer and verification period. They give Urbyte a clean window to estimate usage patterns before any redemption system becomes active.

Is this real redemption?

No. This is a preview conversion tool. Redemption is pending until Urbyte’s backend, verification and partner systems are ready.

What conversion rate is being shown?

This preview uses 1 unused GB = 10 preview DBUs. This is not a final commercial rate and can be adjusted when Urbyte’s model is finalised.

What happens after I convert?

Your preview DBU estimate is saved locally in your browser and shown on the Wallet page. It is not yet a live verified balance.

Can I cash out or redeem after converting?

No. Cash-out, redemption and claim functions are not active during staged rollout.
