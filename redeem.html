Redemption FAQ

Why is redemption pending?

Urbyte is currently validating wallet infrastructure, member verification systems and conversion integrity before opening redemption functionality.

What is the buffer period?

The first 3 billing cycles act as a verification and buffering period. This helps ensure conversions are accurate and prevents artificial balances before redemption activates.

Can I still convert unused data?

Yes. Members can still preview conversions and estimated DBU accumulation using the Convert page.

Are DBUs live yet?

DBUs are currently operating in preview mode while infrastructure and redemption systems are finalised.

Can I withdraw or cash out yet?

No. Withdrawal, redemption, claim and cash-out systems are not active during staged rollout.
