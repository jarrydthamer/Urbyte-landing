Redemption FAQ

Why is redemption pending?

Urbyte is currently validating wallet infrastructure, member verification systems, and conversion integrity before opening live redemption functionality.

What is the buffer period?

The first 3 billing cycles act as a verification and buffering period. This helps ensure conversions are accurate, prevents artificial balances, and allows Urbyte to establish trusted usage patterns before redemption activates.

Can I still convert unused data?

Yes. Members can still preview conversions and estimated DBU accumulation during the buffering period using the Convert page.

Are DBUs live yet?

DBUs are currently operating in preview mode while infrastructure and redemption systems are finalised.

Will members be notified before redemption opens?

Yes. Urbyte members will receive notice before redemption functionality becomes active.

Can I withdraw or cash out yet?

No. Withdrawal, redemption, claim and cash-out systems are not active during staged rollout.
