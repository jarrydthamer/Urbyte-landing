Redemption FAQ

Why is redemption pending?

Urbyte is validating wallet infrastructure, member verification systems and conversion integrity before opening redemption functionality.

What is the buffer period?

The first 3 billing cycles act as a verification and buffering period before redemption activates.

Can I withdraw or cash out yet?

No. Withdrawal, redemption, claim and cash-out systems are not active during staged rollout.
