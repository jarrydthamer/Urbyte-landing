The Real Problem

You are competing
on price. You will lose.

The Australian MVNO market is a race to the bottom. Every carrier resells the same bandwidth. Every MVNO faces the same churn. The only sustainable exit is value differentiation — and nobody has built it. Until now.

01

Price-based differentiation is a dead end

When your cheapest competitor undercuts by $2/month, you lose the subscriber. No loyalty, no switching cost, no stickiness. The churn cycle never ends.

02

MVNOs lease bandwidth — they do not own the infrastructure. Building a data value return product would require carrier cooperation that the carriers will never grant.

03

Optus proved the mechanism works with "Donate Your Data." But authentic value return would expose 30 years of billing asymmetry to class action risk. They cannot move.

Carrier-independent. Out-of-band. No conflict of interest with your wholesale agreement. Your subscribers get real value from their unused data. You get retention and a story.

The Regulatory Tailwind

CDR is coming
to telco. Be ready.

Australia's Consumer Data Right — already live in banking and energy — has telecommunications as its next expansion. CDR gives consumers the legal right to share their usage data with accredited third parties.

Now
Urbyte operates on self-reported data
Subscribers log their plan and usage manually. The platform works today without any regulatory requirement. Traction is being built ahead of the regulatory wave.
CDR Expansion
Automated data feeds replace manual entry
When CDR reaches telco, subscribers will be able to share their usage data directly with Urbyte via accredited API. The calculation becomes automated and instantaneous.
MVNO Advantage
MVNOs have no conflict — carriers do
MVNOs lease bandwidth. They have no structural reason to resist CDR integration. MVNO partners who are already aligned with Urbyte will have a 12–24 month head start on CDR-enabled data value recovery.
