Company summary

What Urbyte is

Urbyte is building consumer-first infrastructure to recognise unused mobile allowance value and convert eligible participation into DBUs™.

What it is not

Not a telco. Not a reseller.

Urbyte does not operate a mobile network, provide carriage services or resell unused mobile data.
