Privacy Policy

Privacy, data handling and consumer trust.

This public privacy policy is written for the corporate website and should be reviewed by a qualified Australian privacy lawyer before final publication.

1. Overview

Urbyte Pty Ltd is building consumer-first digital value infrastructure. This Privacy Policy explains how Urbyte may collect, use, store and protect information provided through this corporate website, contact forms, partner enquiries, media enquiries and related business communications.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how a person interacts with Urbyte, we may collect name, role, organisation, contact details, enquiry details, website analytics and information submitted for vault access, NDA review, pilot discussions or technical assessment.

3. How information may be used

Urbyte may use collected information to respond to enquiries, manage partner discussions, provide media information, assess vault access requests, improve the website, maintain records, support security and meet legal or operational obligations.

4. Partner vault and restricted information

Requests for access to Urbyte's partner vault may require identity, organisation, role and purpose-of-access information. Urbyte may refuse or revoke access where the request is unclear, inappropriate, unauthorised or inconsistent with the company's commercial and security interests.

5. Consumer data position

Urbyte does not operate as a telco, does not provide carriage services and does not resell unused mobile data. Where user-submitted information is requested for future product functions, the intent is to minimise collection, use structured verification, and avoid unnecessary exposure of personal information.

6. Disclosure

Urbyte may disclose information to service providers, professional advisers, technology vendors, regulatory bodies where required, or trusted parties involved in legitimate business operations. Urbyte does not sell personal information to advertisers.

7. Storage and security

Urbyte takes reasonable steps to protect information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

8. Access, correction and deletion requests

Individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by Urbyte, subject to lawful exceptions. Requests can be sent to information@urbyte.com.au.
