How It Works

Four layers.
One outcome.

Urbyte's enterprise stack operates entirely outside your carrier's infrastructure — read-only, no routing latency, no PII exposure.

01
Layer 1 — Telemetry
Urbyte Insight
Urbyte's proprietary telemetry engine connects via read-only, secure mTLS pipelines. It operates entirely out-of-band, processing session-accounting data to isolate the specific network-wide waste delta (Δ) — without touching PII or adding live routing latency.
02
Layer 2 — Verification
Waste Delta Calculation
Every megabyte of verified systemic waste — across retail plans, over-provisioned enterprise fleet pools, and dark off-peak wholesale infrastructure — is isolated, quantified, and independently verified against AASB 138 intangible asset standards.
03
Layer 3 — Minting
DBU Issuance
Verified waste is taken off the open market and minted as Data-Backed Units (DBUs) — closed-loop utility assets at a fixed, transparent rate. Compliant with AASB 138. Every DBU has a deterministic, auditable origin.
04
Layer 4 — Distribution
Givabit Clearinghouse
DBUs route through Givabit directly into DGR-registered Australian charities. For enterprises, this transforms invisible infrastructure loss into an automated, ATO-compliant property donation under TR 2011/4 — delivering an immediate 30% cash tax saving.

Under ATO Tax Ruling TR 2011/4, verified data waste routed through Urbyte's Givabit clearinghouse to a DGR-registered charity qualifies as a property donation — 100% deductible against assessable income.
