Why This Works

Mobile data allowances are contractually pre-paid capacity. When unused, they represent quantifiable intangible value — not a sunk cost. Urbyte applies the same logic that governs software licences, IP portfolios, and digital infrastructure assets.

This is not speculative. It is the application of existing accounting and tax law to a category of asset that has been systematically ignored by enterprise finance teams — until now.
