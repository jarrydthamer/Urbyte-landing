# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does Urbyte sell mobile data?
No. Urbyte is not a carrier or reseller.

## Does Urbyte access communications?
No. Urbyte verifies allocation and usage totals, not communications content.

## What does Urbyte verify?
The eligible difference between telecommunications capacity allocated or paid for and capacity consumed.

## What is a DBU?
An internal accounting unit generated after eligible waste is verified.

## Is Urbyte tied to one network?
No. Urbyte is designed to be network-agnostic.
