How It Works

Simple. Transparent. Yours.

When you join, Urbyte tracks your unused data each month and converts it into credits — redeemable for rewards or donated to Australian charities of your choice.

01
Step 01
Join & Connect

Sign up, select your telco and plan size. Urbyte begins tracking your monthly data gap from day one.

02
Step 02
Watch It Build

Every GB of unused data is captured and converted to DBUs — Data Backed Units — accumulating silently in your account each cycle.

03
Step 03
Direct Your Value

Redeem credits for rewards, gift cards, and experiences — or direct your value to Australian causes that matter to you.

The Pattern

The system was designed to keep it.

Every billing cycle, value accumulates and disappears — not by accident, but by design. Urbyte interrupts that pattern permanently.
