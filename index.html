Urbyte is an independent waste verification platform. Urbyte is not a telecommunications provider, does not resell mobile data, does not claim ownership of unused mobile data, and does not directly access or control your mobile service. DBUs (Data-Backed Units) are internal platform units linked to verified telecommunications waste and are not a guaranteed cash entitlement or financial product.
