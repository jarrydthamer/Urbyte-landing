How It Works

Simple. Transparent.
Yours.

When you join Urbyte, your unused mobile data is tracked and converted into credits — redeemable for rewards or donated to Australian charities of your choice.

01
Step 01
Join & Connect

Sign up, select your telco and plan size. Urbyte begins tracking your monthly data usage from day one.

02
Step 02
Watch It Build

Every GB of unused data is captured and converted to DBUs — Data Backed Units — building in your account each cycle.

03
Step 03
Direct Your Value

Redeem credits for rewards, gift cards, and experiences — or donate directly to Australian charities that matter to you.

The Founder
Jarryd Thamer
Founder & CEO · Urbyte Pty Ltd

"I built Urbyte because I was sick of watching value disappear from people who could least afford to lose it."

Growing up in the Northern Territory and spending years working in construction across Australia, Jarryd experienced firsthand the financial pressures that everyday Australians face. After a period of personal hardship — including homelessness — he channelled that experience into building a platform that fights for the consumer.

Urbyte was built entirely using AI-assisted tools, without a co-founder or external agency, while working full-time in construction. It is a proof of concept for what focused determination and modern technology can achieve.
