Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Urbyte.

Urbyte converts your unused mobile data into rewards credits. Think of them like loyalty points, but backed by something real — the value you've already paid for. Credits can be redeemed for rewards, gift cards, or donated to charity. More details on exactly how this works are coming soon.

You submit your monthly telco bill to Urbyte. We calculate how much data went unused that cycle and convert it into rewards credits. After 3 billing cycles of verification, your balance unlocks for redemption.

Spend them on rewards and gift cards, or donate to Australian charities focused on mental health, homelessness, and domestic violence support. You choose where your value goes.

The 3-cycle window protects you. It lets us verify your actual average unused data before crediting your wallet — so your rewards balance is accurate and fair. Founding Members get their bonus credits immediately; the wait applies to data-backed conversions.

We're building support for all major Australian carriers — Telstra, Optus, Vodafone, TPG, Boost, and more. Join the waitlist and tell us your telco so we can prioritise accordingly.

Yes, anytime. No lock-in contracts. If you cancel, your Founding Member status and any accrued credits remain in your wallet — you just won't receive future monthly bonuses.

Absolutely. Urbyte only reads the data usage figures from your telco bill — we never access your calls, messages, or personal content. All data is handled under the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
